Winter Maintenance Plan Rules

            1. Ground Temperature Thresholds (Section 3)
            Priority 1 treats at ≤ +1.0°C, Priority 2 at ≤ +0.5°C, Priority 3 at ≤ -1.0°C.
          

          

            2. Road Moisture Gate
            Requires precipitation (> 0 mm) or dew point within 1.0°C of ground temperature. Dry sub-zero ground is logged as Don't treat (dry frost).
          

          

            3. Priority 3 Stock Reserve (Section 5)
            Priority 1 routes claim salt first. Priority 3 routes only treat if their depot holds ≥ 40% of full capacity; otherwise Hold back.
          

          

            4. Depot Resource Check & Timing (Section 4)
            Requires sufficient salt and ≥ 1 ready truck/driver. Trucks finish 1 hour before 0°C (1 hr per 20 km), never departing before 17:00.
